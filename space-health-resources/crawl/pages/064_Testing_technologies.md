# Testing technologies

List of Canadian technologies being tested on the Space Station and innovations that could one day be used in space.

Current technology

ACTIS: A tool to assess blood clot risks

Bio-Monitor: A smart shirt that will monitor astronauts' vital signs

Future technology

Canadian Active Neutron Spectrometer: Detecting space radiation in real time

Dextre Deployable Vision System ( DDVS ): An enhanced-vision system for Dextre to inspect the outside of the Space Station

MicroPREP: Lab-on-a-chip technology for sample purification

Past technology

Microflow: A mini portable laboratory that can perform a wide range of bioanalyses

Microgravity Vibration Isolation Subsystem: An isolation system that prevents vibrations from skewing the results of experiments

Bio-Analyzer: A tool that astronauts will use to easily analyze different bodily fluids
